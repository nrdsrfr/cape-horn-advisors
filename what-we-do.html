Early stage and private middle-market firms

  • Define and execute strategies and tactical plans
  • Improve revenues, operations, margins, asset leverage and cash flow
  • Create, build and raise capital for new ventures
  • Position firms to maximize value from exit, succession or third party investment

Firms who invest in or serve privately held firms

  • Angel, VC, Private Equity, Banking, Accounting, Legal
  • Due Diligence prior to acquisition or investment
  • Post transaction integration planning and execution
  • Support to transaction or services being provided
Other middle scale organizations, whether business units of publicly owned firms, governmental or non-profit organizations, who require the same tools we offer to privately held clients
