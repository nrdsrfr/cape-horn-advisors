Cape Horn Advisors is a specialized consulting and advisory practice that provides customized services to meet our clients’ specific needs. We strive to build enduring relationships as a trusted advisor and utilize a highly analytical approach driven by a blend of strategic, financial and operational perspectives and insights to create and execute innovative strategies and solutions for our clients to realize and capture value both near and long term.
To ensure our clients can address the full scale of needs, we have established collaborative partnerships and working relationships with other well established business consulting and advisory organizations who offer complementary services to what we provide.
We have done this to ensure that we deliver the right level of expertise, skills and resource to meet clients’ unique needs with the highest quality of service in everything we do.
We are open and forthright in expressing when we are not the appropriate resource to address a client’s specific needs. In such cases we are happy to provide referrals, step away or collaborate with any firm a client has engaged to lead an engagement or address certain issues.
