Cape Horn Advisors is a specialized niche consulting and advisory practice that provides services customized to meet client’s specific needs. We strive to build enduring relationships as a trusted advisor and offer a highly analytical approach driven by a unique blend of strategic, financial and operational perspectives and insights that enable us to create and execute innovative strategies and solutions for realizing and capturing value for our clients both near and long term.
To address the full scale of client needs, Cape Horn has established collaborative partnerships and working relationships with other well established business consulting and advisory organizations who offer services that complement what we provide.
We have done this to ensure that we deliver the right level of expertise, skills and resource to meet clients’ unique needs with the highest quality of service in everything we do.
We are open and forthright in expressing when we are not the appropriate resource to address a client’s specific needs. In such cases we are happy to provide referrals, step away or collaborate with any firm a client has engaged to lead an engagement or address certain issues.
